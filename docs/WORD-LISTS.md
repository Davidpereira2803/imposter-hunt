# Word lists

The setup picker groups topics into **Quick pick**, **Luxembourg**, **Browse categories**, and **Your topics**. Luxembourg contains place and culture prompts; Luxembourgish contains words, foods, traditions, and local expressions. Category cards show translated names, word counts, icons, and a selected state. Random picks a category uniformly, then a word from that category, so a large list does not crowd out smaller ones.

The common word packs are localized for French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Luxembourgish. Food, animals, countries, sports, occupations, colors, clothing, vehicles, instruments, and technology use language-specific prompts (roughly 12–20 per category). Movie titles, brands, and Luxembourg place names remain in their familiar proper-name forms. The English lists remain the broadest; translated packs are curated to common, easy-to-recognize prompts.

Built-in lists should use distinct, familiar prompts. When expanding one, remove duplicates and avoid entries that are too similar to guess fairly. The Luxembourgish list is intentionally a mixed prompt list rather than a vocabulary course; regional variants are marked by their source context where relevant.

## Luxembourgish references

The Luxembourgish vocabulary includes everyday translations checked against the [Luxembourgish Online Dictionary (LOD)](https://lod.lu/), such as [Paangech](https://lod.lu/artikel/PAANGECH1), [Int](https://lod.lu/artikel/INT1), [Bei](https://lod.lu/artikel/BEI1), and [Déierendokter](https://lod.lu/artikel/DEIERENDOKTER1). The local word list also includes foods checked against the dictionary, including [Bouneschlupp](https://lod.lu/artikel/BOUNESCHLUPP1), [Gromperekichelchen](https://lod.lu/artikel/GROMPEREKICHELCHEN1), [Boxemännchen](https://lod.lu/artikel/BOXEMANNCHEN1), [Kachkéis](https://lod.lu/artikel/KACHKEIS1), [Kaweechelchen](https://lod.lu/artikel/KAWEECHELCHEN2), [Quetschentaart](https://lod.lu/artikel/QUETSCHENTAART1), [Kniddel](https://lod.lu/artikel/KNIDDEL1), and [Wäinzoossiss](https://lod.lu/artikel/WAINZOOSSISS1). The official Luxembourg portal also documents local dishes and traditions such as [Hämmelsmarsch and Kiermes](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/society-and-culture/festivals-and-traditions/funfair-haemmelsmarsch.html), [Schueberfouer](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/society-and-culture/festivals-and-traditions/schueberfouer.html), and [Buergbrennen](https://luxembourg.public.lu/en/society-and-culture/festivals-and-traditions/buergbrennen-bonfire-carnival.html).

The list retains regional vocabulary with care: [Pimpampel](https://infolux.uni.lu/paeiperlek-pimpampel-co/) is documented as a regional word for butterfly, and [Seejomes](https://infolux.uni.lu/klengt-gedeiesch-iwwer-ameisen-a-seejomessen/) is attested in University of Luxembourg dialect research as a locally popular variant for ant. The list keeps these regional forms alongside more widely used vocabulary.
